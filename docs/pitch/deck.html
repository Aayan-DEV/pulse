Problem

Busy stores plan blind.

  • While travelling in Oslo, I met my old Domino's employer. He now owns a very busy Burger King near Aker Brygge.
  • His real problem is planning ahead around what happens near the store: trains that stop, events, weather, and why one day went well.
  • I lived it too. At Domino's there was no forecast. When a rush hit, I was called in at short notice to help for a few hours.
It is a personal problem, not a guess.

Solution

Pulse watches the area for you.
