Busy stores have a blind spot.

  • I went back to my old Domino's.
  • I talked with the owner, who also owns the Burger King at Aker Brygge.

His problems

  • Not knowing why his stores make sales.
  • Not knowing what happens nearby in real time, like trains that stop working.

Mine

  • At his Domino's, I was called in when a rush hit.
  • Often the rush was over when I arrived. Lost sales, unhappy customers.

Solution: the Palantir for
high-demand retail stores.

  • Watches the area around a store live: events, trains, weather.
  • An hourly forecast of orders, the crew it needs, and why.
  • Every decision comes from matching live events against the store's sales data.
  • Built and simulated on one real Burger King in Oslo.
  • Any store with an address can get the same local picture.

What I built in 48 hours.

Model

  • A LightGBM model forecasts orders for every hour: a low, a likely and a high number.
  • 30% fewer mistakes than a simple average, tested on a year it never saw.
  • Runs inside the app, checked against the Python original.

Live data

  • MET Norway Locationforecast and Frost observations.
  • Entur Journey Planner, live vehicles, and 1.8 million past stop calls (SIRI-ET on BigQuery).
  • Venue programmes scraped live with context.dev.

Agents and staffing

  • Gemini on Vertex AI: an event scout with Google Search grounding, a road camera watcher, a staffing overseer.
  • Erlang C crew per hour and a set-covering shift plan (linear programming).
  • 38 tests, including Norwegian Working Environment Act rules.

FAQ

01What's your next step if you win tonight?

Launch it live and talk to the customers booked for Monday. Then connect a real till and train on real sales.

02What's your biggest technical risk?

The model learned from simulated sales. I only know the real accuracy once a till is connected.

03What did you leave out, and why?

Till connection, logins and more stores. I made one store work end to end first.

04How will you get your first 100 users?

Talk to busy fast food stores in Oslo, and in Finland, where I live.

05How have you tested this with users?

Not yet. The first customer calls are booked for Monday.

06What legal hurdles could stop this?

None. All data is public, or the owner's own store data.

07Why hasn't anyone solved this before?

Tools forecast from past sales only. They don't see what is happening around the store right now. That is the part I built.
