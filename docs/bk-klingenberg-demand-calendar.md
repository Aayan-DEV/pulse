# Burger King Klingenberggata 5: demand and staffing calendar

Researched on 8 Nov 2026. The period covered is Nov 2026 to Dec 2027.

## How to read the ratings

- **Impact** runs from 1 to 5. A 5 means the event can make or break a day's stock and staffing.
- **The arrow** shows which way it pushes:
  - ↑ means more customers.
  - ↓ means fewer customers.
  - ⇅ means the mix of customers changes (who comes, and when).
  - $ means it changes your costs.
- **Distance** is measured from the restaurant door.
- **TBD** means the date has not been published yet.
- **Inferred** means the date follows the event's yearly pattern but has not been announced.

## Why this spot behaves the way it does

- **Transit hub.** You are about 200 m from Nationaltheatret station.
  - In 2025 it had about 8.8 million T-bane boardings, roughly 24,000 a day.
  - All 8 local train lines stop there, plus the airport train.
  - Every crowd heading to Ullevaal or Holmenkollen, and many heading to Fornebu, passes your door.
- **The cinema next door.** Klingenberg kino seats 766 in its main hall, and Saga kino has 6 screens.
  - A blockbuster release fills both.
  - People come in before and after the films.
- **Karl Johan, Spikersuppa and Rådhusplassen.** Every parade, the Christmas market and the Nobel events happen within 400 m.
- **Nightlife.** Bars must stop serving by 03:00, and you close at about 04:00 on Fridays and Saturdays.
  - The late-night spike after the bars close is a fixed weekly event.
  - For safety, put your most experienced crew on it.
- **Your hours** are 10:00 to 24:00 Monday to Thursday, 10:00 to 04:00 Friday and Saturday, and 11:00 to 24:00 Sunday.
- **Your main competitor nearby** is Max at Egertorget (Karl Johans gate 18C).

## Impact 5: plan these days individually

1. **Thu 10 Dec 2026: Nobel Peace Prize** ↑
   - Navi Pillay receives the prize at Rådhuset (350 m) at 13:00.
   - The torchlight march goes down Karl Johan and ends at Grand Hotel (150 m) at about 17:30 to 18:00.
   - 2026 is the prize's 125th anniversary.
   - Plan for a large evening wave.
2. **7 Nov 2026 to 3 Jan 2027: Jul i Vinterland at Spikersuppa (next door)** ↑
   - The market is open every day except 24 Dec.
   - The ice rink opens around 30 Nov.
   - This lifts every afternoon and evening for 8 weeks.
   - It peaks on weekends and during the Christmas school holiday (21 Dec to 1 Jan).
3. **Mon 17 May 2027: Constitution Day** ↑↑
   - About 60,000 children march down Karl Johan from 10:00 to 13:30. It is the largest parade in the country.
   - The same day is Whit Monday, and possibly Eid al-Adha (16 or 17 May).
   - This is the biggest day of the year. Staff at the maximum from opening, and order extra of everything.
4. **20 to 26 Jun 2027: Oslo Pride** ↑
   - The parade on Sat 26 Jun goes along Karl Johan to Kontraskjæret and Rådhusplassen.
   - In 2026, 90,000 people walked in it. One older count puts spectators at about 200,000.
   - Pride Park at Rådhusplassen (400 m) runs all week.
5. **Fri 18 Jun 2027: Take That at Tjuvholmen (about 1 km)** ↑
   - This is an open-air concert at 18:00 and the band's first ever show in Norway.
   - The crowd walks past you before and after.
   - It is also the last school day before the summer holiday.
6. **12 to 21 Mar 2027: Holmenkollen** ↑
   - The FIS ski weekend is 12 to 14 Mar, with the men's 50 km on the 13th and the women's on the 14th.
   - The Biathlon World Cup finale is 15/16 to 21 Mar.
   - Tens of thousands of fans take T-bane line 1 from Nationaltheatret.
   - Expect a morning rush before the races and a hungry crowd coming back in the afternoon.
   - Eliteserien also starts on 14 Mar.
7. **Norway men's football at Ullevaal (about 27,000 people)** ↑
   - **Sat 14 Nov 2026:** Norway vs Wales, 17:00 or 18:00.
     - The same day has The Prodigy at Unity Arena and Synne Vo at Oslo Spektrum.
     - This is the busiest crowd day in the next month.
   - **Euro 2028 qualifiers (TBD):**
     - The draw is on 6 Dec 2026.
     - Home games are expected in the windows 26 to 30 Mar, 11 to 15 Jun, 23 Sep to 5 Oct and 11 to 16 Nov 2027.
     - Check the fixtures right after the draw.
   - **Sat 29 May 2027:** the men's cup final (NM-finalen), probably at Ullevaal. Weezer plays Oslo Spektrum the same evening.

## Impact 4: plan the week

8. **T-bane closures through the city centre** ↓ ⇅
   - During these no metro trains run through the centre: Nationaltheatret, Stortinget and Jernbanetorget all have no T-bane service.
   - Replacement trams run from Majorstuen to Jernbanetorget.
   - The dates:
     - **19 to 22 Nov 2026 (Thu to Sun).** This clashes with Marstein (20 Nov) and Westlife (21 Nov, sold out) at Oslo Spektrum.
     - **25 to 28 Feb 2027**
     - **26 to 29 Mar 2027 (Easter)**
     - **4 to 7 May 2027 (Ascension)**
     - **1 to 4 Jul 2027**
     - **12 to 15 Aug 2027 (Øya)**
     - **Four days in Sep 2027, TBD**
   - What it means: fewer commuters change trains near you, but more people walk and take trams on the street.
   - The net effect is unknown, so measure your own sales on 19 to 22 Nov.
9. **Train closures (Bane NOR)** ↓
   - **25 Jan to 5 Feb 2027:** no trains between Oslo S and Lysaker on weekday nights (22:30 to 04:15). This hurts late shifts and staff who commute by train.
   - **30 to 31 Jan 2027:** closed the whole weekend.
   - **17 Jul to 3 Aug 2027:** Oslo S closed to trains. This is reported but not yet verified.
10. **Big film releases at Klingenberg kino and Saga kino** ↑
    - **18 Dec 2026:** Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three are both scheduled worldwide. The Norwegian dates are not confirmed.
    - The release lands on the first Friday of the Christmas holiday, with the Christmas market next door.
    - Staff up for that weekend.
11. **Christmas peak and quiet days** ↑ then ↓
    - **Black Friday is 27 Nov 2026.** Shops open on the Sundays of 6, 13 and 20 Dec.
    - **"Halv skatt" (half tax) in November or December.** People keep more of their pay that month, so they spend more.
    - **24 Dec:** shops close by 16:00. Very quiet in the evening.
    - **25 Dec and 1 Jan:** very low demand. Decide whether to open at all.
    - **31 Dec:** there is no city fireworks show, and private fireworks are banned inside Ring 2. Expect a normal-to-quiet evening and then a busy night after midnight.
12. **Cruise ships, May to Sep 2027** ↑
    - Ships dock 1 to 1.5 km away, a 10 to 15 minute walk from Karl Johan.
    - The busiest days are 3 and 4 Jul (8 ships each) and 22 and 26 Jul (7 ships each).
    - About 151 ship calls are scheduled in July 2027.
    - Busy days bring a lunch rush of tourists, so plan simple and fast menus.
    - Oslo plans a NOK 100 per passenger cruise tax in 2027. This is a small risk that fewer passengers come.
13. **Ramadan, about 8 Feb to 8 Mar 2027** ⇅
    - Lunch drops among Muslim customers.
    - There is a rush after sunset (iftar), which is around 17:00 to 18:00 in February.
    - Expect a late-night lift on weekends.
    - Eid al-Fitr is about 9 or 10 Mar, which is a big family day out.
    - Check halal status with King Food. I found no public statement.
14. **Wage costs and staffing rules** $
    - Since 6 Nov 2026 the legal minimum wage applies to every employer in the sector:
      - Adults start at NOK 215.29 an hour.
      - At 18: NOK 176.84.
      - At 17: NOK 162.58.
      - Under 17: NOK 153.08.
    - If you are bound by the collective agreement (for example through NHO Reiseliv membership), you also pay these extras per hour:
      - Evening: NOK 16.59
      - Saturday from 14:00 and Sunday: NOK 31.52
      - Night: NOK 58.04
    - Night shifts on Friday and Saturday until 04:00 are your most expensive hours.
    - Workers under 18 and done with ungdomsskolen (lower secondary school):
      - No work from 21:00 to 06:00.
      - At most 8 hours a day.
      - No overtime.
    - The night rule rules out using minors for closing shifts. One exception may allow work until 24:00; get a lawyer to confirm.
    - Adults must get 11 hours' rest between shifts. Rotating someone from a close at 04:00 to an open at 10:00 breaks that rule.
    - **From 1 Jun 2027** you must pay sick pay in advance for up to 4 months. This affects cash flow.

    - Oslo Marathon: about 11 Sep, start and finish at Rådhusplassen. Roads close in the morning, then hungry runners arrive.

20. **Paydays** ↑
    - Lånekassen pays student loans on the 15th. The big semester payments arrive around 5 Jan and 5 Aug.
    - State employees are paid on the 12th, and many private employers pay around the 20th.
    - Expect stronger weekends right after these dates.
21. **Weather** ⇅
    - Rain and snow move orders from the counter to Wolt. Studies show more hot-food delivery orders in cold weather.
    - November is the wettest, darkest month, so shift labour toward delivery packing.
    - Warm sunny days from May to August raise footfall on Karl Johan.
    - There are about 80 snow days a year.
22. **Demonstrations at Stortinget (150 m)** ⇅
    - Oslo kommune counts more than 700 a year. Most are small.
    - The big marches are 8 March and 1 May.
    - Large events can close streets but also bring crowds.

## Impact 2: watch, but rarely changes a shift

23. **Supply**
    - **Beef:** Norway is short on beef in 2026 and covers the gap with imports.
    - **Bird flu:** autumn migration brings a risk to chicken and egg supply. Check Veterinærinstituttet.
    - **Potatoes:** the 2026 harvest is 20% higher, so fries are safe.
    - **Strikes:** strikes at ASKO and REMA were avoided in 2026.
24. **Prices and taxes for 2027**
    - VAT stays the same: 15% on takeaway and 25% when eating in.
    - The Storting may still cut food VAT in the budget deal.
    - The sugar tax and the bottle packaging tax rise slightly.
    - Restaurant prices are up 6% over the last year.
    - The policy rate is 4.5% and may go higher. That squeezes household budgets, which can push customers toward cheaper fast food.
25. **Wage negotiations and strikes**
    - Spring 2027 is a smaller interim wage round.
    - A 6-week restaurant strike happened in 2026 (Burger King was not affected).
    - Strikes in transport and food supply are the risks to watch.
26. **Wolt couriers' legal status**
    - The Supreme Court will decide whether Wolt couriers count as employees.
    - If they do, delivery fees and commission rates could change.

## Impact 1: background

27. **Packaging rules**
    - A new Norwegian packaging regulation is proposed. Some single-use portion packs may be banned later.
    - Single-use plastic bans are already in force.
28. **Rare events**
    - A royal or state event can close the centre, as King Harald's funeral did on 9 Sep 2026.
    - Use that day and the summer 2026 station closure as test data from your own sales history.

## Data gaps to close yourself

1. Your own sales history, hour by hour, for past event days. This is the best predictor you have.
2. Whether you are bound by the collective agreement, which decides whether you pay the evening, weekend and night extras.
3. Whether the Wolt delivery hours match your opening hours. Wolt appears to stop around 23:30 on weekdays.
4. Who owns King Food. The sources conflict: some say Umoe, others say Ring International since 2020.
